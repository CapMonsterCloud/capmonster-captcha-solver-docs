# Bonuses and loyalty program

CapMonster Cloud offers various reward programs for new and regular users, users of other ZennoLab products, as well as developers and software owners.

## <img src={newUserBonus} alt="" width="50" height="50" style={{ verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '1px' }} />For new users

After registering with CapMonster Cloud, you can receive **$0.1 to your balance to test the service**.

To receive the test funds:

1. Register with [CapMonster Cloud](https://dash.capmonster.cloud).
2. Contact [technical support](https://helpdesk.zennolab.com/en-US/conversation/new) or email us at [support@capmonster.cloud](mailto:support@capmonster.cloud).
3. Specify the email address associated with your account.

:::warning Important:
The test bonus is not granted to accounts registered using a temporary email address.
:::

---

## <img src={loyaltyBonus} alt="" width="50" height="50" style={{ verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '1px' }} />For regular users

Regular users can receive **up to a 15% bonus when topping up their balance**.

The bonus amount depends on the amount spent on CAPTCHA recognition in CapMonster Cloud over the last 30 days:

| Spending over the last 30 days | Bonus on balance top-up |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------: |
| from $500                      |                      5% |
| from $1,000                    |                     10% |
| from $10,000                   |                     15% |

Once the corresponding level is reached, the bonus is automatically applied to each balance top-up and remains active for **30 calendar days**.

### How the last 30 days are calculated

A rolling 30-day period is used for the calculation rather than a calendar month.

This means that spending is calculated not from the first to the last day of the month, but for the 30 days preceding the current date.

For example, if you started actively solving CAPTCHAs on **August 16**, then on **September 16** the system will check your spending for the previous 30 days. If you reached one of the bonus levels during this period, the corresponding bonus will be applied to your balance top-ups.

After reaching a bonus level, the bonus is automatically applied to each balance top-up and remains active for **30 calendar days**.

If your spending reaches the next level during this period, the bonus amount will be increased automatically.

## <img src={zennolabProductsBonus} alt="" width="50" height="50" style={{ verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '1px' }} />For users of other ZennoLab products

If you already use ZennoLab products, such as **ZennoPoster**, **ZennoDroid**, or other company products, you can receive **$5 to your CapMonster Cloud balance**.

The bonus is credited automatically if the following conditions are met:

* you have a ZennoLab account;
* you previously made a purchase or subscribed for more than **$5** in the [ZennoLab personal account](https://account.zennolab.com/personal-area-main/my-products);
* you sign in to CapMonster Cloud using the same ZennoLab account.

For example, if you previously purchased a ZennoPosterPro subscription for one month, six months, or one year, and then sign in to CapMonster Cloud for the first time using the same credentials, the bonus will be automatically credited to your CapMonster Cloud balance.

:::info One account for ZennoLab and CapMonster Cloud
Accounts on `account.zennolab.com` and `capmonster.cloud` use a single account.

If you already have a ZennoLab account, you do not need to register separately with CapMonster Cloud. Sign in to CapMonster Cloud using the same credentials or the same Google account.

If you try to register again using an email address that is already in use, the system will inform you that the email address already exists.
:::

*For detailed instructions on connecting CapMonster Cloud to various programs, see [How to connect CapMonster Cloud to the required program?](./external-services/external-program.mdx)*

## <img src={developerBonus} alt="" width="60" height="60" style={{ verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '1px' }} />For developers and software owners

Developers and software owners can receive **up to 30% of the CAPTCHA-solving spending of referred users**.

Partnership terms are determined individually.

To learn which terms are available to you:

* fill out the form on the [CapMonster Cloud website](https://capmonster.cloud/en/);
* email us at [support@capmonster.cloud](mailto:support@capmonster.cloud);
* or contact [ZennoLab technical support](https://helpdesk.zennolab.com/en-US/conversation/new).
