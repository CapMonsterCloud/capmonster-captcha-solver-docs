## Volume-Based Bonuses

Earn a bonus added to your gift balance for every deposit, based on the volume of CAPTCHAs solved in CapMonster Cloud. The bonus is valid for 30 calendar days from the moment the conditions are met:

* From $500/month: 5% bonus
* From $1,000/month: 10% bonus
* From $10,000/month: 15% bonus

## Starter Deposit for New CapMonster Cloud Users

New CapMonster Cloud users receive a small starter balance $0.1, sufficient to test the service on your real-world tasks. To claim it, please contact [technical support](mailto:support@capmonster.cloud?subject=Starter%20deposit%20for%20a%20new%20CapMonster%20Cloud%20user).

## Bonus for New ZennoPoster Pro Users

Anyone who purchases [ZennoPoster Pro](https://zennolab.com/en/products/zennoposter/) receives a $5 bonus for testing CapMonster Cloud. This deposit is enough, for example, to solve 5,000 reCAPTCHA v2 Enterprise challenges or roughly 3,800 Cloudflare challenges.

The bonus is credited automatically.
