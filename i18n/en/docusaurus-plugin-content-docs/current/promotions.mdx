## Bonuses for the Volume of Solved Tasks

Receive a balance bonus based on the volume of CAPTCHAs solved in CapMonster Cloud during the past month*. The bonus is credited as soon as the conditions are met:

* from $500/month: 5% bonus
* from $1000/month: 10% bonus
* from $10000/month: 15% bonus

For example, you started solving CAPTCHAs on the 16th. One month later, we evaluate the volume of CAPTCHAs you solved during the previous period. If the promotion conditions are met, the corresponding bonus is credited to your balance.

\* *A month is considered to be 30 calendar days*

## Initial Deposit for New CapMonster Cloud Users

New CapMonster Cloud users receive a small initial balance of $0.1, which is sufficient to test the service on your real tasks.

To receive the bonus, please contact [technical support](https://helpdesk.zennolab.com/en-us/conversation/new) and specify the email address associated with your account.

## Bonus for New ZennoPoster Pro and ZennoLab Product Users

Those who purchase [ZennoPoster Pro](https://zennolab.com/en/products/zennoposter/) receive a $5 bonus for testing CapMonster Cloud.

Moreover, users of [ZennoLab](https://zennolab.com/en/) products who make their first purchase of more than $5 will also receive the same bonus.

The bonus is credited automatically and only if you have not previously used the CapMonster Cloud service.
